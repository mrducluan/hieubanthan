# Refresh and measurement

## Refresh protocol
- Keep the slug stable. Dated, time-bound content (lưu niên, current-year transits) lives in a labelled section so only that section is rewritten.
- Review on a schedule suited to the page, quarterly for timing-heavy pages and yearly for evergreen ones. Each review: update the timing section and FAQ with new queries from Search Console, fix broken links, re-run the validator, bump `dateModified` only when content really changed.
- Change the substantive core only when a source or fact was wrong. Keep a short change note.

## Measurement
Per cluster, track: impressions, clicks and average position of the page's real queries (Search Console), internal link clicks from hub to spokes, and whether the page ranks for the queries in its question pool.

AI citation spot check, monthly: run the page's 5-10 core questions in the answer engines you care about, log whether the site is cited and which passage was used, and strengthen the passages that get ignored. Treat results as directional; engines vary run to run.

## Pruning
A page with no impressions after a full quarter, or whose intent duplicates another page, is merged into the stronger URL with a 301, not left to compete.
